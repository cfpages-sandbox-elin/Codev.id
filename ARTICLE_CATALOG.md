# Article Catalog — codev.id

Scope: 120 evidence-led briefs for the company-level software-development knowledge layer of `codev.id`. The catalog covers discovery through decommissioning and represents existing website, UI/UX, WordPress, custom development, content, redesign, repair, maintenance, and Cloudflare claims without treating promotional copy as proof.

Domain boundaries: root `codev.id` owns cross-cutting software-development education and substantiated company/service evidence. Existing commercial routes retain transaction intent. `domain.codev.id`, `automate.codev.id`, `gadget.codev.id`, `invest.codev.id`, `iot.codev.id`, `pools.codev.id`, `tools.codev.id`, and `whois.codev.id` retain their product features, documentation, help, pricing, and cases after separate audits. No location-swapped brief is allowed.

Catalog statistics: **20 parent topics**, **120 briefs**, **6 briefs per topic**. First wave: **12 P0 assets**. The other waves expand system depth only after evidence, reviewers, implementation capacity, and first-wave measurements are available.

Priority legend: `P0` establishes the first bounded authority cluster; `P1` covers high-value decisions and assurance; `P2` adds implementation and operational depth; `P3` is evidence-intensive or lower-frequency coverage. Wave labels are bounded publication groups, not simultaneous production.
